a.  If you insert this sequence of points: p(i) = (1/(2^i), 1/(2^i)), it will maximally unbalance the tree and cause it to degenerate into a list.
    An O(1) queury would be inserting point (0.1, 0.9) since it would go to an empty quadrant and an O(n) queury would be inserting point (1, 1) which would 
    traverse the whole branch.
b.  When you insert the 9th identical point, the leaf exceeds capacity of 8 and splits into children. Since all leaves have the same point they all move into the same
    child and split again. This keeps on happening until we run out of memory. A solution would be to set a maximum depth and allow leaf nodes at that depth to hold more than 
    8 primitives.
c.  The translation may cause objects to move across quandrants and rotating could change which quadtrant contains which object. Quadtrees are better suited for scenes
    where few objects and they dont move too much. They aren't suited for super dynamic scenese since even small transformations can can change the structure of the 
    tree and make it expensive to update.
